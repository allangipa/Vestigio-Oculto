The Dyatlov Pass incident

Sealed Archive · Urals, USSR, 1959 Published

Sverdlovsk regional Procuracy, 1959 · public domain · cropped

Dead9
Tentcut from the inside

Nine skiers left their tent in the dark, almost all of them without boots. The Soviet inquiry closed the case in 1959, attributing the deaths to an elemental force, and filed the records away. In 2020 the Russian Prosecutor's Office concluded it was an avalanche, a 2021 model showed that one is possible — and in June 2026 the families again asked for a new case.
