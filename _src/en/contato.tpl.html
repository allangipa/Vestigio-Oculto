Contact

Get in touch with Vestígio Oculto

A correction, an image credit, a request about your data or anything else: there is only one way in.

E-mail allangipa@gmail.com

01What to write about

  • Corrections. A wrong date, name or number in a dossier. Send the source along: that is what makes a quick correction possible.
  • Images and credits. If you are the author of an image used here and the credit is incomplete, or you want it removed, write.
  • Your data. Requests under the LGPD, Brazil's data protection law, as described in the privacy policy.
  • Story ideas, press and partnerships. Topic suggestions are also welcome.

02How we reply

There is no form and no sign-up: the conversation is by e-mail, and your address is not used for anything other than replying. A confirmed correction goes into the dossier.
