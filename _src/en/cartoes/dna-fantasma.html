A human lineage known only through statistics. In 2020, a model estimated that West African populations carry 2% to 19% inheritance from a group that split from ours hundreds of thousands of years ago — with no bone at all. In 2023, another model explained the same signal without it. The dossier separates the data from the interpretation.
