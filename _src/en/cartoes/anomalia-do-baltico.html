The Baltic Sea anomaly

Discovered Yesterday · Gulf of Bothnia Published

Image NASA/GSFC, MODIS · public domain · cropped

Sonar2011
Depth≈ 85 – 91 m

A circular shape on the seabed, found by chance. A survey in search of shipwrecks returned a sonar image too round to ignore. The stones that came up from the bottom were granite, gneiss and sandstone with an iron crust; the internet read something else. The dossier separates the sonar's echo from the reading of the echo — and tells what the 2025 expedition has not yet published.
