Denisovans

Discovered Yesterday · Altai, Siberia Published

Photo Александр Байдуков · CC BY-SA 4.0 · cropped

Identified in2010
Froma finger bone

An entire human lineage was in a fragment of a finger bone. It was not a skull or a skeleton: it was the DNA of a piece of bone in a Siberian cave that revealed a group that lived alongside us — and whose genetic material many people still carry today.
