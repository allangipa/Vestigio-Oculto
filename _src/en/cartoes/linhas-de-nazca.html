The Nazca Lines

Stone and Dust · Pampa de Nazca, Peru Published

Photo Diego Delso · CC BY-SA 4.0 · cropped

Extent≈ 450 km²
Figures893 known

Drawings too large for their makers to see — or almost. Figures and straight lines scraped into the desert between about 500 BC and AD 500. The large ones seem to have been paths to walk; hundreds of small figures, found with artificial intelligence since 2019, sit beside trails and can be read from the ground.
