The heads of Rano Raraku have whole bodies under the ground. The quarry still holds hundreds of half-finished moai. How they were moved, why they stopped being made and what really happened to the population are three distinct questions — and the research of 2024 and 2025 answered each of them differently from the version that became a best-seller.
