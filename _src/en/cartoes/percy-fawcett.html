Deaths in the searchesone, not a hundred

He was looking for a city he called “Z.” A British colonel went into the Upper Xingu with his son and a friend, and the forest closed behind the three of them. A hundred years of searches produced bones that were not theirs, rumors that do not add up and no answer.
