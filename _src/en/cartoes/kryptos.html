A sculpture in the CIA courtyard holds a text no one has deciphered. Jim Sanborn cut four encrypted messages into a copper sheet. Three fell by 1999. The fourth, 97 letters long, was found in a Smithsonian archive in 2025, sold for $962.5 thousand and locked inside a checker — but the method remains unknown.
