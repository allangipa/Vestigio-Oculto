Puma Punku

Stone and Dust · Altiplano, Bolivia Published

Photo Benjamin Burga · CC BY-SA 4.0 · cropped

Construction≈ AD 580 – 710
Andesite blocks≈ 150, none in place

Pieces that fit together as if they had been mass-produced. Andesite H-blocks, sandstone slabs weighing dozens of tons and bronze cramps cast into the stone itself, at almost four thousand meters. An unfinished temple, dismantled over five centuries — and the stones refute, one by one, the thesis that someone from outside built it.
