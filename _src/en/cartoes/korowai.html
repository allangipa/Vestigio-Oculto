The house in the treetop exists — and today it is almost always built to order. Thirty years of photographs turned a rare dwelling into the portrait of an entire people. The BBC admitted it had filmed a house built for the cameras; ethnography tells another story, about witchcraft, new villages and tourism — and in 2026 the Korowai area became a zone of armed conflict.
