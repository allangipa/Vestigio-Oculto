Göbekli Tepe

Stone and Dust · Şanlıurfa, Turkey Published

Photo Philrock · CC BY-SA 4.0 · cropped

Age11,600 years
Excavated≈ 10% of the site

The enclosures did not collapse: they were found full. Hunter-gatherers raised limestone pillars five and a half meters tall on top of a hill without water — six thousand five hundred years before Stonehenge, before agriculture and pottery. Centuries later, everything vanished under debris. Schmidt read a ritual burial there; the team digging today sees the hillside giving way.
