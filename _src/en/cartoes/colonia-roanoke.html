A settlement abandoned its site, and left word of where it was going. When the supply ship came back, three years late because of the war with Spain, the houses had been taken down and CROATOAN was carved on a post. In 2026, two teams announced they had found the colonists — in two different places, and neither with a peer-reviewed paper.
