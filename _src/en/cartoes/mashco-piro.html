The Mashco Piro people

The Last Isolated · Madre de Dios, Peru Published

Estimated population≈ 750

They are appearing on the riverbanks — and that is a symptom. Descendants of those who fled the rubber massacres, the Mashco Piro live in voluntary isolation between Peru and Acre. In 2024 they appeared in front of villages and shot at loggers; in September 2026 the Inter-American Court heard the case. Each sighting measures the pressure on the forest, not their curiosity.
